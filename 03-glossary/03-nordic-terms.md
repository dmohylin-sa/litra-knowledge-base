# Nordic terms - words you may hear in the room

> **In short:** Litra's people will speak English with you, but their systems, documents and habits are Norwegian and Swedish. Recognising these words helps you follow screenshots, file names and side remarks.

> **Use with care:** these are common industry words, not an official dictionary. Usage varies between companies, so confirm with the client when a word matters for a requirement.

## Useful context

- **NOK, SEK, DKK, EUR** - the four currencies the platform must handle (Finland uses EUR).
- **Statens vegvesen** (NO) and **Trafikverket** (SE) are the road authorities. **DSB** in Norway oversees dangerous goods.
- **EHF** is the Norwegian electronic invoice format. Sweden uses PEPPOL-based e-invoices too.
- **Æ, Ø, Å** (Norwegian) and **Å, Ä, Ö** (Swedish) must display and sort correctly in every screen, report and export.
